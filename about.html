About Glory Kids

Raising a generation of children who encounter Jesus, know His Word, and carry His presence into everyday life.

A Note From Jandré

Hi, I'm Jandré — a pastor, children's ministry leader, and the heart behind Glory Kids. More than anything, I love Jesus. And everything you'll find here flows from that place.

With more than 12 years leading children's and family ministry, I've seen firsthand what happens when kids don't just learn about Jesus, but actually encounter Him.

I believe the next generation isn't just the future of the Church — they are the Church right now. And they deserve more than surface-level teaching. They deserve to truly encounter Jesus… to know His voice, to love His Word, and to walk daily with the Holy Spirit.

That's why Glory Kids exists.

I create Bible lessons, resources, and curriculums that are not just informative, but transformational — helping kids, families, churches, and schools experience the presence of God in a real and lasting way.

My heart is to equip you — whether you're a parent, teacher, or pastor — with everything you need to disciple children with confidence, depth, and joy. Because when a child encounters Jesus, everything changes.

And when families build their lives on His Word, generations are impacted.

Welcome to Glory Kids. 🤍

— Jandré, Founder
Glory Kids Ministries
Our Mission

Why Glory Kids Exists

Glory Kids Ministries exists to raise a generation of children who encounter Jesus, know His Word, hear His voice, walk in the power of the Holy Spirit, and carry His presence into everyday life.

We equip churches, leaders, and families with Scripture-centered, Spirit-filled resources that help children not simply learn about God, but genuinely know Him and follow Him.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

✝️ Jesus at the Center
Everything begins and ends with Jesus. Our greatest goal is not simply to raise children who know Bible stories, but children who know, love, and follow Jesus.
📖 Scripture as Our Foundation
We believe the Bible is the inspired Word of God and the foundation for what we teach and how we live. Encounter never replaces Scripture; genuine encounter leads us deeper into God's Word and into obedience to Jesus.
🔥 Encounter, Not Just Information
Children are capable of genuinely knowing and experiencing God. We create space for children to respond to what they are learning through prayer, worship, listening, reflection, and practical activation.
🕊️ The Presence of God
We want children to grow in an everyday awareness of God's presence — not only at church, but at home, at school, with friends, and wherever they go.
✨ Life With the Holy Spirit
We believe the Holy Spirit is active in the lives of children. He reveals Jesus, helps us understand Scripture, transforms us to become more like Christ, gives spiritual gifts, and empowers us to live courageously for God.
💪 Faith That Becomes Action
Following Jesus changes how we live. We encourage children to pray, worship, obey, serve, forgive, show compassion, share their faith, and demonstrate God's love to the people around them.
👑 Children Can Carry the Kingdom
Children are not merely the Church of tomorrow — they are part of the Church today. We believe God can speak to them, work through them, and use their lives to impact their families, schools, communities, and the world.
🏡 Church and Family Together
Discipleship is bigger than a Sunday lesson. We equip children's ministry leaders, churches, and parents to work together in helping children build a lifelong relationship with Jesus.
🎨 Joy, Creativity & Wonder
Learning about God should awaken wonder. We use storytelling, creativity, music, play, imagination, conversation, and age-appropriate experiences to help biblical truth become memorable and meaningful.
Statement of Faith

What We Believe

We believe in one God — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — eternally existing as three Persons.

We believe the Bible is the inspired and authoritative Word of God, revealing who God is, His purposes for humanity, and the good news of Jesus Christ.

We believe Jesus Christ is the Son of God, fully God and fully human. He lived a sinless life, died on the cross for our sins, rose bodily from the dead, ascended to the Father, and will come again.

We believe humanity was created in the image of God but has been separated from Him through sin. Salvation is God's gift of grace, received through repentance and faith in Jesus Christ, not something we can earn.

We believe the Holy Spirit lives in believers, revealing Jesus, transforming us into His likeness, helping us understand God's Word, empowering us to live holy and courageous lives, and giving spiritual gifts for the building up of the Church and the advancement of God's Kingdom.

We believe God still speaks and works today in ways that are consistent with Scripture. We believe in prayer, healing, prophecy, spiritual gifts, and the supernatural work of the Holy Spirit, always centered on Jesus and tested according to God's Word.

We believe children can genuinely know God and participate in the life of His Kingdom. They can pray, worship, hear God's voice, respond to the Holy Spirit, serve others, share their faith, and grow as disciples of Jesus.

We believe in the Church as the family and people of God, called to worship Jesus, make disciples, love one another, care for those in need, and carry the gospel to the world.

We believe Jesus calls us to love God wholeheartedly and love others, and that a life transformed by Him should increasingly demonstrate His character, compassion, holiness, justice, generosity, and love.

Above everything, we believe Christianity is not merely about knowing things about God. We were created to know Him.

Our desire is to see a generation of children who encounter Jesus and carry His presence.
