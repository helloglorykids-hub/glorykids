Most of us were never taught how to help a child hear God

When a child says "I think God told me something," most of us freeze. We don't want to shut down their faith. We also don't want them confused, or chasing feelings instead of knowing Jesus. That is why the series is built around three things:

📖 Scripture first

Every activation is weighed against who God has revealed Himself to be in His Word.

❓ Questions over answers

Kids learn discernment when we ask "What do you think God might be saying?" instead of telling them.

🕊️ Safe space to respond

Kids practice listening and responding to Jesus without pressure to perform.
