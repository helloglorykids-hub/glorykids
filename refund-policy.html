Refund Policy

What you can expect if something isn't right with your purchase.

Last updated: 5 September 2026

Glory Kids Ministries sells digital products through glorykidsministry.com. Because our products are delivered instantly and cannot be returned, this policy explains when a refund is available.

1. Glory Kids Membership — Billing & Auto-Renewal

Glory Kids Membership is a recurring subscription billed through PayFast. Monthly plans renew automatically every month; annual and church plans renew automatically every 12 months, at the price shown when you subscribed, until you cancel. We do not send a separate reminder before each renewal.

Cancelling. You can cancel any time from your dashboard (Membership → Cancel membership) or by emailing us. Cancelling stops all future charges. Your access continues until the end of the period you have already paid for; we do not pro-rate or refund the remainder of a paid period.

14-day money-back guarantee

New memberships come with a 14-day money-back guarantee. If you are not satisfied, contact us within 14 days of your first payment and we will cancel your membership and refund that payment in full — no questions asked.

  • The guarantee applies to your first payment on a new membership only.
  • Renewal payments (monthly, annual or church) are not covered by the guarantee, but you can cancel at any time to stop future charges. Cancel before your renewal date to avoid being billed for the next period.
  • We may decline a repeat guarantee request where an account has previously been refunded and re-subscribed.

2. One-Time Digital Downloads & Curriculum Packs

Individual lesson packs, curriculum bundles, and other one-time digital purchases are non-refundable once the files have been downloaded or accessed, because the product cannot be returned. Please review the product description and any preview before buying.

3. When We Will Always Help

Regardless of the above, contact us and we will make it right if:

  • You did not receive your download link or cannot access your files.
  • The files are corrupted, incomplete, or materially different from what was described.
  • You were charged twice for the same item, or charged after cancelling.
  • You believe a payment on your account was not authorised by you.

4. How to Request a Refund

Email hello.glorykids@gmail.com from the address on your order, with your order number and a short description of the issue. We aim to respond within 2 business days.

5. How Refunds Are Paid

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method through our payment provider (PayFast). It can take 5–10 business days for the funds to appear, depending on your bank or card issuer.

6. Your Statutory Rights

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you may have under the South African Consumer Protection Act or other applicable law.

7. Contact

Questions about refunds? Email hello.glorykids@gmail.com.
