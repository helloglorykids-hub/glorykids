The Glory Kids
12-Month Curriculum

Encounter Jesus. Carry His Presence.

Glory Kids exists to raise a generation that encounters Jesus, hears His voice, experiences His power, and carries His presence. Twelve complete monthly series — Voice, Power, Presence — woven into one intentional discipleship journey. Included free with Glory Kids Membership, launching September 15, 2026.

The Curriculum DNA

Built on Three Pillars

This curriculum isn't a collection of Bible stories. Every lesson helps children know God personally, understand Scripture, encounter Jesus, respond to the Holy Spirit, and live out their faith.

🗣️
VOICE
Hear God. Know His Word. Follow His leading.
Children discover that God speaks through Scripture and that the Holy Spirit leads God's people. They learn to listen, discern what agrees with God's Word, and respond to Him.
⚡
POWER
Trust God. Live courageously. Participate in what He is doing.
Children discover God's supernatural power and learn that the Holy Spirit empowers ordinary people to pray, serve, obey, demonstrate God's love and live courageously.
🕊️
PRESENCE
Know Jesus. Be with Him. Become like Him. Carry Him everywhere.
Children develop a personal relationship with God through prayer, worship, encounter and awareness of His presence, with the goal of becoming more like Jesus.
Flexible by Design

Join Any Month of the Year

Every month is a complete, standalone 4-week series. A church doesn't need to complete October before November, or January before February — but when all 12 series are followed together, they form one intentional discipleship journey.

Structured, but not dependent
Progressive, but modular
Deep, but simple enough for children
Supernatural, but biblically grounded
Fun, but never shallow
